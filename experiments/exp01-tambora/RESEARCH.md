# Research & verified facts — Experiment 01 (Tambora → the first bicycle)

Method: web search (standard mode), cross-checked across independent sources. Where sources
disagreed or only hedged, the script uses hedged wording or leaves the claim out.

## Claims used in the script

| Claim in script | Status | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Mount Tambora (Sumbawa, Indonesia) erupted in April 1815, VEI 7, most powerful eruption in recorded history | Verified (several sources agree; main climactic event dated 10 April 1815) | [History Today](https://www.historytoday.com/node/53633), [Scientific American](https://www.scientificamerican.com/blog/history-of-geology/april-10-1815-the-eruption-that-shook-the-world/), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1815_eruption_of_Mount_Tambora) |

No period price or head-count figures were found, so **none are used**. | [BW state PDF](https://beteiligungsportal.baden-wuerttemberg.de/fileadmin/redaktion/m-mvi/intern/Dateien/PDF/PM_Anhang/161021_Fahrrad_Jubilaeum_Geschichtlicher_Hintergrund.pdf), [Mannheim flyer](https://www.mannheim.de/sites/default/files/page/220/drais_flyer_web.pdf), [99% Invisible](https://99percentinvisible.org/article/the-running-machine-volcano-inspired-forerunner-to-the-modern-bicycle/) |

## Deliberately NOT used
- "Oats cost eight times more" — one blog figure, no primary source.
- "Frankenstein was conceived because of the weather" — a good story, but a second idea; the film keeps one idea.
- Tambora death tolls — sources disagree wildly (36,000 to ~100,000).
- "Forester" for Drais — he was a forester but was released from duty in 1810 to invent, so the script says "inventor".
- Richter-scale figure for Tambora (one source confuses VEI with Richter).

## Correction applied during research
The first hook said "one side of the planet / the other side". Tambora and Mannheim are ~12,100 km apart — far, but not antipodal (that would be ~20,000 km). The hook now says "one corner of the world … another".
